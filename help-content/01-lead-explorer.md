# Lead Explorer — Help Center Collection

**Collection description (for the collection card):**
> Find, enrich, and organise B2B leads from a database of 840M+ verified profiles.

Each `## Article N` section below is one Intercom article. UI labels are bolded exactly as they appear in the product.

## Article 1 — What is Lead Explorer?

**Lead Explorer** is SalesTarget.ai's lead database and enrichment tool. It gives you access to a large verified B2B dataset so you can build targeted prospect lists and unlock verified contact details without leaving the platform.

### What you get

- **840M+ individual profiles** — decision-makers, executives, and professionals across every industry
- **90M+ business records** — company data including size, revenue, industry, and tech stack
- **On-demand enrichment** — unlock verified emails, phone numbers, and company intelligence only for the leads you actually want

### Who it's for

Lead Explorer is built for sales, marketing, and growth teams who need to:

- Find the right prospects without manual research
- Unlock verified emails and phone numbers for people already on their list
- Build targeted lists filtered by role, industry, company size, location, and more
- Push qualified leads straight into an outreach campaign

### How it fits into your workflow

Lead Explorer is the starting point. Once you've found and enriched your leads, you can send them directly into:

- **Campaigns** — to launch automated email sequences
- **LinkedIn campaigns** — to run connection and message campaigns

Leads you contact through a campaign then flow into your **CRM** automatically, with their engagement history attached.

### Where to find it

Click **Lead Explorer** in the left sidebar.

## Article 2 — How to Search for People in Lead Explorer

People Search lets you find individual professionals from a database of **840M+ verified profiles**, filtered by job title, seniority, location, and more.

### Step 1: Open People Search

1. Click **Lead Explorer** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **filter panel on the left**, switch to the **People** tab.

The **Company** tab is selected by default, so you'll need to switch to **People** each time you start a new people search.

### Step 2: Apply your filters

Use the filter panel on the left to define your target audience.

**Person filters**

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| Full Name | Search for a specific person by name |
| Job Title | Search by title or keyword |
| Seniority | 16 levels including C-Suite, VP, Director, Manager, Owner, Founder, Partner, and Non-managerial |
| Department | The team or function the person sits in |
| Email | Look up a specific email address |
| Phone Number | Look up a specific phone number |
| LinkedIn Profile | Look up a specific LinkedIn URL |

**Location filters**

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| Country | Filter by one or more countries |
| State / Region | Narrow to a state or region |
| City | Narrow to a specific city |

**Company filters**

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| Company Name | Target people at a named company (with an **Exact Match** option) |
| Industry | The company's industry |
| Company Size | 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1001-5000, 5001-10000, 10001+ |
| Company Revenue | Fixed revenue bands |

**Availability toggles**

Turn on **Has Email** or **Phone Number** to only return people where that data can be unlocked.

**Pro tip:** Start with 2-3 broad filters, check the result count, then narrow down. Most filters also support **exclusions** — add a value as an exclusion to remove those results instead.

### Step 3: Run your search

Click **Search**. Results appear as a table in the centre of the screen with these columns:

- Name
- Email
- Phone Number
- Title
- Company
- Location
- LinkedIn
- Department

Email and phone values are **masked** until you unlock them — you'll see `***@***.com` with a **View Email** or **View Phone** button in the cell. Hover the button to see the credit cost before you spend anything.

### Step 4: Work with your results

- Use **Edit Columns** to choose which columns are visible.
- Use the results-per-page selector at the bottom to show 20, 50, or 100 results per page (20 is the default).
- Select leads with the row checkboxes. To go beyond the current page, use the **Select all {N} results** option in the selection banner, or choose a custom number of leads.
- The **Total** and **Saved** chips at the top show your live result count and how many leads you've already saved.

Once you've selected leads, you can enrich them, add them to a list, or push them into a campaign — see the articles that follow.

## Article 3 — How to Search for Companies in Lead Explorer

Company Search lets you find and research **90M+ business records** worldwide. Use it to identify target accounts, research prospects, or build account-based lists.

### Step 1: Open Company Search

1. Click **Lead Explorer** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **filter panel on the left**, make sure the **Company** tab is selected. It's selected by default.

### Step 2: Apply your filters

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| Company Name | Search by name, with an **Exact Match** option |
| Domain | Find a company by its domain |
| Website URL | Match on a specific website |
| LinkedIn URL | Match on a company's LinkedIn page |
| Industry | The company's industry category |
| Company Size | 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1001-5000, 5001-10000, 10001+ |
| Company Revenue | Fixed revenue bands from 0-500K up to 10T+ |
| Country / State / Region / City | Location filters, applied separately |
| Tech Stacks | Find companies using specific technologies |
| Intent Topics | Companies actively researching a topic (Bombora data) |
| Business Events | 40+ trigger events such as new funding round, IPO announcement, or leadership change, with a 30/60/90-day lookback |

**Business Events are the strongest buying signal available in Lead Explorer** — a company that just raised funding or changed leadership is far more likely to respond than one picked on firmographics alone.

### Step 3: Review your results

Results appear as a table. Company Name and Domain are always visible; the remaining data sits in enrichment column groups you can unlock:

- **Firmographics** — revenue, country, headquarters, employees, website, LinkedIn, industry, description, ticker, locations
- **Technographics** — the company's tech stack
- **Financial metrics**

Locked values show a **View …** button with the credit cost on hover.

### Step 4: Find people at a company

Click **View People** on any company row. This switches you to the **People** tab with the search already filtered to that company, so you can find the right contacts immediately.

### Step 5: Save companies

Select the companies you want and click **Add To List**.

> **Important:** companies must be **enriched** before they can be added to a list. If you select unenriched rows you'll see "Enrich leads first" — run enrichment on them, then add them.

## Article 4 — How to Enrich Leads in Lead Explorer

Enrichment is how you unlock the data that's hidden behind a **View …** button — verified emails, phone numbers, company intelligence, and more. You only spend credits on the leads you choose.

There are two ways to enrich.

### Option A: Unlock a single value

Click the locked button directly in the results table:

- **View Email** — unlocks the verified email address
- **View Phone** — unlocks the phone number
- **View Profile** — unlocks the professional profile
- **View Posts** — unlocks recent LinkedIn activity

Hover any button first to see exactly what it costs. The value replaces the button as soon as it's unlocked.

### Option B: Enrich in bulk with the Enrichments drawer

1. Select the leads you want using the row checkboxes.
2. Click **Enrichments** in the action bar.
3. In the drawer, tick the enrichment types you want.
4. Check the **Credit Impact** preview — it shows your current balance and what it will be afterwards.
5. Click **Apply Enrichments**.

Large jobs run in the background. You'll see progress as leads are enriched, and you can keep working while it runs.

### What it costs

Each enrichment type has its own price, and the total is the price per type multiplied by the number of leads. See **Enrichments & Credits — Complete Guide** for the full price list.

**You are never charged twice.** If a lead has already been enriched, re-running that enrichment costs nothing. And if no verified data can be found for a lead, **no credits are deducted** for that lead.

### Checking your balance

Your credit balance is always visible in the **Total Credits** widget at the top right of the screen. Click it to see the breakdown between ST Credits and Email Credits.

### If a button is greyed out

Enrichment types can be restricted by role. If you see a **No access** tooltip, your account doesn't have permission for that enrichment type — ask your workspace owner or admin.

### What's next

Once leads are enriched you can:

- **Add To List** — save them into a named list
- **Add to Campaign** — push them into an email campaign
- **Add to LinkedIn** — push them into a LinkedIn campaign

## Article 5 — How to Save Leads to a List in Lead Explorer

Lists let you organise leads into named groups you can reuse, export, or push into campaigns.

### Before you start

**Leads must be enriched before they can be saved to a list.** If you select unenriched leads you'll see "Selected leads are not enriched yet." You can either enrich them first, or use the **Enrich & Save** option in the Add To List dialog to do both at once.

### Save leads to a list

1. Select the leads you want using the row checkboxes.
2. Click **Add To List** in the action bar.
3. In the dialog, either:
   - Choose an existing list from **Select Existing List**, or
   - Choose **Create New List** and name it.
4. Click **Add To List** (or **Enrich & Save** if the leads still need enriching).

### Saving more than one page of results

You don't have to save page by page. When you tick the header checkbox, a banner appears offering:

- **Select all {N} results** — selects every lead matching your current filters, across all pages
- A custom option to select the first N results

This is resolved against your filters on the server, so it works no matter how many pages of results you have.

### Finding your lists

There are two places lists appear, and they're different:

- **Lists** in the left sidebar — the full Lists page, with tabs for **Uploaded Lists**, **Lead Explorer Lists**, and **Company Lists**
- **My Lists** panel inside Lead Explorer — a quick side panel for saving into while you search

### Managing a list

From the Lists page you can:

- **Rename** a list
- **Download** it as a CSV
- **Delete** it
- **Move** or **Copy** leads between lists

Removing a lead from a list does **not** delete the lead — it stays available in Lead Explorer and in any other list it belongs to.

People lists and company lists are stored separately.

## Article 6 — How to Export Leads from Lead Explorer

You can download your saved leads as a **CSV** file at any time — useful for sharing with your team, importing into another tool, or working offline.

### Export a saved list

**From the Lists page**

1. Click **Lists** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the list you want.
3. Click **Download** on that row.

The download starts immediately. For large lists (1,000+ leads) the file is prepared as a background job — you'll see progress and get a notification when it's ready.

**From inside Lead Explorer**

When you're viewing a saved list in Lead Explorer, click **Download CSV**.

### Choosing what's in the file

There is no field picker at download time. The CSV contains **whichever columns are currently visible** in your table, so set them up first:

1. Click **Edit Columns**.
2. Turn on the columns you want in the export and turn off the ones you don't.
3. Apply, then click **Download CSV**.

Lists downloaded from the **Lists** page include all stored fields for those leads.

### Does exporting cost credits?

**No.** Downloading your lists doesn't consume any credits — you've already paid for the data at the point of enrichment. In fact, your **first export earns you bonus credits**.

Only enrichment costs credits. Once a lead is enriched, you can export it as many times as you like at no extra cost.

### Formats

CSV only. There is no Excel or PDF export — CSV opens directly in Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers.

## Article 7 — How to Send Leads to Your Campaigns

Once your leads are saved and enriched, you can push them straight into an email or LinkedIn campaign without exporting and re-importing.

### Send leads to an email campaign

1. Open a list from **Lists**, or view a saved list in Lead Explorer.
2. Select the leads you want.
3. Click **Add to Campaign**.
4. In the dialog, search for and select the campaign you want. If you don't have one yet, use **Create New Campaign** to make one inline.
5. Click **Add Selected (N)**.

You'll be taken to the campaign editor so you can review the sequence.

**Field mapping is automatic** — you don't map anything. Lead fields are matched to your personalization variables for you.

> **Leads without an email address are skipped automatically.** The dialog shows you how many will be added and how many will be skipped before you confirm, so enrich your leads with an email first.

### Send leads to a LinkedIn campaign

1. Select the leads you want.
2. Click **Add to LinkedIn Campaign** (shown as **Add to LinkedIn** inside Lead Explorer).
3. Pick the LinkedIn campaign.
4. Click **Add to LinkedIn (N)**.

The dialog automatically filters to leads that have a LinkedIn URL — anyone without one is left out.

### Adding leads while inside a campaign

If you open Lead Explorer from within a campaign, you'll get a dedicated **Add to Campaign** button that targets *that* campaign directly — handy when you're topping up an existing sequence.

### What about the CRM?

There's no "send to CRM" step, and you don't need one. Leads you contact through a campaign flow into your CRM automatically, with their engagement history — opens, clicks, and replies — already attached.

## Article 8 — Enrichments & Credits — Complete Guide

This is the full reference for what each enrichment gives you and what it costs.

### How credit pricing works

Each enrichment type has a fixed price per lead. When you enrich in bulk, the cost is:

**Price per enrichment × number of leads**

So enriching 10 leads for both email and phone costs (2 + 5) × 10 = **70 credits**.

Two rules always apply:

- **Already-enriched data is never charged again.** Re-running an enrichment on a lead you've already unlocked costs nothing.
- **No data, no charge.** If no verified result can be found for a lead, you aren't charged for that lead.

### People enrichments

| Enrichment | Credits | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Email Address | 2 | Verified work email address |
| Phone Number | 5 | Direct phone and mobile number where available |
| Professional Profile | 1 | Expanded professional and career details |
| Individual's Social Media Presence | 5 | Recent LinkedIn activity — post count, average likes and comments, days since last post |

### Company enrichments

| Enrichment | Credits | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Firmographics | 1 | Size, revenue, industry, headquarters, description, locations |
| Financial Metrics | 1 | Financial indicators for public companies |
| Funding & Acquisitions | 1 | Funding rounds, investors, and acquisition history |
| Website Keyword Search | 1 | Searches the company's website for keywords you specify |
| Technographics | 2 | The company's tech stack |
| Webstack (BuiltWith) | 2 | Technologies detected on the company's website |
| Website Traffic | 2 | Website traffic estimates |
| Employee Ratings | 2 | How the company is rated by its employees |
| Workforce Trends | 2 | Hiring and headcount trends |
| Company Hierarchy | 2 | Parent, subsidiary, and branch relationships |
| Company Social Media Presence | 2 | The company's recent LinkedIn activity |
| Intent Data (Bombora) | 3 | Topics the company is actively researching, with an intent score |
| Lookalike Companies | 5 | Companies similar to the target |

Public companies also have dedicated enrichments — **Business Challenges**, **Competitive Landscape**, and **Strategic Insights** (2 credits each), drawn from their 10-K filings.

Exact prices for every enrichment are shown in the **Enrichments** drawer before you confirm, along with a **Credit Impact** preview showing your balance before and after.

### What's free

You don't need credits to search. Every search returns a free preview:

**People** — Name, Title, Company, Location, LinkedIn, and Department, with email and phone masked until unlocked.

**Companies** — Company name, industry, size range, revenue range, domain, location, and LinkedIn.

All filters are free to use too, including seniority, company size, and revenue.

### Notes on specific enrichments

- **Website Keyword Search** requires you to enter the keywords you want to search for.
- **Intent Data (Bombora)** lets you optionally set a minimum intent score (0-100) to only return companies above a threshold.
- **Social Media Presence** covers **LinkedIn activity** specifically.

### Checking your balance

Your balance is always visible in the **Total Credits** widget at the top right. Click it for the breakdown between ST Credits and Email Credits. The Enrichments drawer also shows your remaining balance and what it'll be after the job.

## Article 9 — How to Unlock Emails and Phone Numbers

Email and phone are the two enrichments most people use every day, so they're worth covering on their own. They're **separate enrichments** — you choose and pay for each independently.

| Enrichment | Credits per lead | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Email Address | 2 | Verified work email |
| Phone Number | 5 | Direct phone and mobile where available |

Unlocking both for the same lead costs **7 credits**.

### Unlock for one lead

In the results table, click **View Email** or **View Phone** in that lead's row. The value replaces the button immediately.

### Unlock in bulk

1. Select the leads with the row checkboxes.
2. Click **Enrichments**.
3. Tick **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, or both.
4. Review the **Credit Impact** preview.
5. Click **Apply Enrichments**.

### Verified only — or nothing

SalesTarget.ai doesn't guess or generate email addresses from patterns. You either get a verified address or nothing at all, and **you aren't charged when nothing is found**.

### Improving results

- Leads with a complete profile (full name, current company, LinkedIn) return verified data more often.
- Use the **Has Email** and **Phone Number** availability toggles in the filter panel to only search people whose data can be unlocked. This is the single best way to avoid wasted effort.
- For lists you've imported from elsewhere, run them through **Lead Validation** (in the left sidebar) before adding them to a campaign.

### After unlocking

Enriched leads can be added to a list, exported to CSV, or pushed into an email or LinkedIn campaign. Exporting them later costs nothing extra.

## Article 10 — How to Search with AI

If you'd rather describe your ideal customer than set filters one by one, use **Search with AI**.

### How it works

1. Open **Lead Explorer**.
2. Click **Search with AI**.
3. Describe who you're looking for in plain English, for example:
   - "VPs of Sales at SaaS companies in the US with 50-200 employees"
   - "Marketing directors at healthcare companies in London"
   - "Founders of fintech startups that recently raised funding"
4. Run the search.

The AI translates your description into the same filters you'd otherwise set by hand, then runs the search.

### Refining the results

AI search is a starting point, not a replacement for filters. After it runs, the filter panel shows what it selected — you can adjust anything from there:

- Add or remove a filter the AI missed
- Widen a filter if the result count is too small
- Add exclusions to remove results you don't want

### Bulk search

If you already have a list of companies or people, use **Bulk Search** to paste them in — separated by commas, semicolons, or line breaks — and match them against the database in one go. This works with company names, domains, website URLs, and LinkedIn URLs.

### Recent searches

Your recent searches are saved in a dropdown at the top of the search bar, so you can jump back to a previous set of filters without rebuilding it.
